How this mode works
  1. A customer selects tests and completes the Curex booking form.
  2. Curex saves the booking in the local sales workspace.
  3. The customer is opened into WhatsApp with the booking details pre-filled.
  4. Your team receives and manages the booking manually in WhatsApp Business.
  5. Staff can open the saved booking in Curex, update payment/status, and convert it to a sale.

No Meta developer account, webhook, public HTTPS endpoint, Cloud API token, or Phone Number ID is required for this assisted workflow.
